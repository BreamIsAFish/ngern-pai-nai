# Features

- **Income, expenses, and transfers.** Create, edit, and delete THB entries. Income and expense entries support categories, tags, and notes.
- **Monthly ledger.** Browse entries by local calendar month and refresh them from Google Sheets.
- **Monthly summary.** Compare income and expenses, then group entries by category or tag.
- **Thai receipt import.** Add up to 10 receipt photos at once using your own OpenAI or Google AI Studio API key. Valid receipts become uncategorized expenses immediately; duplicates and failures are shown separately and are never inserted.
- **Search.** Find entries by category, tag, note, destination, or amount across the latest 12 months or a selected year.
- **Categories and tags.** Use built-in categories or manage your own categories, icons, and tags.
- **Private Google Sheet storage.** Sign in with Google and keep the app's data in your own Drive.
- **Spreadsheet recovery.** Restore the app's spreadsheet from Drive trash or create a replacement.
- **Private AI settings.** Select OpenAI or Google AI Studio, store each API key in device secure storage, choose from the provider's fixed model list, and validate the selection before importing.
